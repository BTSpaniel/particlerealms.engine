# SOUL civilization gaps and founder camp roadmap

SOUL now supports a separate founder camp with no permanent homes, assigned occupations or authored daily routines. This page distinguishes the delivered browser implementation from the learning and long-duration civilization evidence still needed. Existing furnished villages remain supported.

## Balance basic abilities with learned choices

Provide working bodies, navigation, reach checks, carrying, interaction and resource owners. Let each person choose among currently available proposals and learn from their own consequences. An adult does not need to rediscover limb control before gathering food or experimenting with a tool.

| Layer | Delivered foundation | Learning or evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Body and world | Stable identities, one world/two views, actual routes, collision, carrying, placement and owner receipts | Broader motor skills, deliberate route efficiency and perceptual transfer |
| Personal behavior | Empty initial procedure memory; exploration, repeated outcome statistics, revisions, private RNG and delivered teaching | Reliable multi-step planning and transfer across novel contexts |
| Resources | Finite sites, water/food, tool wear/custody, replacement tools, repair, storage, exchanges and accepted deliveries | Regrowth, farming, refining, weather and sustainable production |
| Homes | Resident proposals, bounded dimensions, one/two floors, private beds, shared cooking/living space, material-backed stages and invitations | Demonstrated learned design quality, broader building programs and settlement planning |
| Society | Mutual relationships/families, care, fighting, theft, local commitments, communities and ballots | Unforced durable division of labor, successful family care and several generations of survival |
| Neural learning | WebGPU candidate training, reviewed generic decision records, held-out gates and separate serving checkpoint | A competent observation-dependent civilization policy and broad conversation |

The engine decides whether an action is possible and records its actual result. Personal outcome learning can value actor benefit, harm, relationships and commitments differently. A completed theft or fight is not automatically labelled desirable or approved for shared training.

## Delivered scenarios and workflow

A fresh app opens paused with 100 founders and no model allocation. Project setup offers new populations of 2, 8, 32 or 100. Eight is a useful engineering test size, not a research-derived optimum. Creating a camp first archives the prior complete project. **Exports → Download previous project** makes that archive available for import. Existing village imports preserve their homes, authored daily lessons and utility controller.

The actual starter kit contains two shared stone axes, two shared picks, a workbench, a basket and loose bedroll per person, and two food units/two litres of water per person in a shared depot. It does not currently include crops, a farm, a saw, a fire/weather system or a renewable forest. Hand gathering branches, loose stone and fibre can supply a replacement axe without requiring an existing axe. A one-axe scarcity scenario is still a future separately balanced experiment.

Founder needs and navigation work without a home. Resource observations require personal visibility; remembered stock can become stale. Accepted delivery requests communicate a meeting location rather than granting access to the requester's hidden live position. A child starts with fresh personal discovery, including when the family has no permanent house.

**Play society** uses `personal-outcomes-v1`. Optional serving-model participation adds a small WebGPU-scored shortlist and labels the combined controller explicitly. It does not start training. Available proposals are bounded by kind and rotated targets so a crowded scene cannot permanently displace shelter actions with earlier social/resource options. The authors still define possible actions, recipes, need effects and proposal affordances; this is not independent invention of arbitrary programs.

## Shared literature and personally delivered learning

The default library implements a scarce starting collection: 38 titles and 42 physical copies on two shared founder shelves or eight selected household shelves in the 100-person furnished village. It retains the supplied plan’s 100 fictional example passages and adds short complete original first editions, including 100 actual microstories. Titles are not empty promises, and fictional diaries, registers and chronicles never become invented resident histories.

Reading is an actual object/task effect with copy, edition, receipt and tick provenance. Only readers acquire the text. Local teaching can carry that passage onward with a bounded delivery lineage; it grants no fabricated practice or neural competence. The operator catalogue, personal records and physical copies are distinct projections. Durable drafts and publishing preserve old reading editions while updating existing copies, without creating a copy per person or approving a shared training record.

Following evidence still needed: whether residents voluntarily finish useful books, retain their content, relate passages to observed situations, teach accurately, and improve held-out decisions after reviewed WebGPU training. Semantic text access currently bypasses optical page perception; OCR, learned reading comprehension, physical pages, spontaneous authorship and resident printing/copy production remain separate work. The 256-passage personal limit and 16-link teaching limit are explicit engineering bounds.

## Personal procedures and truthful outcomes

Each individual retains at most 64 single-action procedures, 32 independent sequence variants and 128 recent episodes. Saved personal RNG makes the next exploration choice reproducible without other people's choices advancing that RNG. Physical-action contexts in `soul.skill-conditions.v2` include relevant needs, inputs, capacity, target state and required tools. Unrelated goods and need bands no longer fragment those procedures. Other actions retain conservative full contexts; the exact-context baseline preserves its original behavior.

The `terminal-dependencies-v2` bank stores each sequence's steps, starting conditions, independent receipt traces, attempts, successes, failures and observed return. A new prefix cannot replace another variant's support. Sequences contain at most four steps and project relevant later needs into their starting conditions. A variant becomes executable after two personal completions, a completion fraction of at least 60% and positive mean observed return. Rejection or interruption contributes the actual partial return and failure evidence. These thresholds are engineering policy settings, not calibrated success probabilities.

Dependency extraction starts at the terminal owner effect and follows only its material or tool prerequisites. The production owners record actual `consumedInputs` for crafting, repair, delivery and shelter work. The learner checks inventory deltas and accounts for intervening spending; tool prerequisites require acquisition, crafting or repair followed by measured wear. A shared commitment, passive energy recovery or nearby timestamps cannot establish a chain. Need, material and tool effects retain their measured values, including negative intermediate effort; there is no fabricated completion bonus.

Locally delivered teaching copies a procedure or relevant sequence variant with explicit teacher provenance. Newly taught procedures and variants start with zero recipient trials, outcomes and receipt evidence. Existing personal knowledge stays intact when a new sequence arrives. A bounded teacher prior can influence a single-action choice. An applicable taught sequence may now enter explicitly labelled practice at most twice, with an eight-personal-decision cooldown between starts. The recipient must choose a currently available first action, and every later step still requires an available owner action and its real result. Practice has separate start, completion and failure counters; ordinary learned execution still requires two verified personal traces and the existing return/completion thresholds. The controller uses only the individual's supplied observations and own state. The operator dashboard remains a separate read-only projection.

Admission, work completion and cancellation have distinct receipts. Rest cannot run productive work concurrently. Work can pause, survive restore and resume under its original receipt; cancelled work cannot become a success. Manual conflicting actions cannot overwrite a pending personal action. Personal travel does not send an authored greeting and does not create a duplicate Founder-owned shared lesson. Restore validates active sequence references, receipt counts, step identities and receipt chronology, alongside pending evidence and the experience outbox. Legacy sequence records remain historical; active legacy executions enter recovery when the revised semantic controller initializes its sequence bank.

Immediate procedure updates are episodic/statistical JavaScript state. Neural weight updates require reviewed datasets and explicit browser training. All neural forward passes, loss, backpropagation and optimizer updates use WebGPU.

## Resources, construction and placement

### Next physical interaction: doors

The 1 October audit confirms that residents cannot yet open or close doors. SOUL's furnished cottage compiles a fixed open leaf into its shell groups and does not request RealmForge's optional mechanism generation. Founder shelters currently have open doorways. General rendering and ForgeSource compilation do not supply an executable action for every object.

Build the connection in this order:

1. Add a versioned mechanism-enabled SOUL template with separately bound leaf, handle, hinge and frame identities; preserve legacy fixed-open projects.
2. Expose only personally observed interaction points and state. Admit approach/reservation through the existing actor-task owner, including reach and occupancy checks.
3. Request opening or closing through `RealmForgeConstructionPresetPhysics.setArticulationOpening`; retain its locked, busy and blocked outcomes. Use measured articulation state as completion evidence.
4. Publish the same leaf pose and collision state to both views and invalidate affected navigation. Refuse travel through an unavailable or still-closed passage.
5. Save mechanism state and pending task identity. Test interrupted opening, competing people, obstruction, locked doors, restore and stale observation without duplicate effects.
6. Record storylets from those actual outcomes, then teach and evaluate choosing the interaction. Expected success is a hypothesis until observed.

Reuse `RealmForgeActorUseRuntime` for its supported approach/use contracts rather than duplicating them. Its current 16-actor bound and the mechanism runtime's serialized active request require explicit scheduling and population tests. Extend the same pattern to storage lids, taps, switches and appliances only when their physical owners are connected. Storylet prose and dictionary definitions cannot operate missing mechanisms.

The founder economy owns finite spatial sites, distinct tool ownership/custody, carrying capacity, observed workstations, reservations, work progress and conservation ledgers. Gathering, chopping and mining require actual reach and elapsed work. Recipes and repairs reserve real inputs; outputs appear only when completed and able to fit. Borrowing, tool theft, local exchange and accepted delivery use the same committed owner paths. Accepted construction work exposes its next material deficit, which can inform available requests and gathering without assigning a fixed job sequence.

Shared `AIEconomy` station, capacity and production-destination defects identified during the audit were corrected and covered by regression tests. SOUL still owns its separate physical production transactions; a recipe helper alone does not supply durability, hauling, ownership or exactly-once world effects.

Shelter designs currently validate 4–12 m width, 6–12 m depth, one/two floors, one to three sleeping rooms per floor and a private sleeping room per resident. Staged wood/stone/fibre costs install the actual generated pieces. Two-floor fixtures exercise physical stairs, upper-floor beds and mid-stair restoration. These bounded assemblies are compiled through RealmForge; they do not modify RealmForge's general text resolver to accept arbitrary apartment or condominium programs.

Founder shelter stage/part counts depend on the proposed design. The legacy furnished village's 12-stage, 2,075-piece cottage and prepared stock for 28 expansions remain a separate system. Founders begin without that prefab stock or those houses.

Movable bedrolls and supported furniture preserve IDs, geometry, interaction anchors and restored placement. Structural pieces remain construction-owned. A support cannot be moved while another object depends on it; the dependent object must be removed first. Cooking fixtures and water inventory do not imply plumbing, utilities or a simulated fire system.

## Live civilization reporting

The operator dashboard has a searchable/sortable population table and a selected-person panel. It separates current activity, accepted work and role, and reports health, energy, hunger, thirst, inventory, tool custody/condition, family, relationships and personal procedures. Resource tables distinguish carried, stored, reserved and natural quantities. Construction, local offers and retained events remain inspectable.

Rendering coalesces committed updates and retains keyed rows, selection and focus. Runtime reporting exposes nested training metrics and labels the decision budget per tick. Model allocation is not total GPU VRAM. The population benchmark uses only cohort sizes that exist in the project; unavailable/busy people are counted separately, and founder replays do not inject authored routines.

The operator now retains bounded 10-second samples, per-person deprivation and outcome counts, resource production/consumption and 600-second work windows. Historical food runway, mortality and migration still require additional models and measurements. Current incapacitation is temporary, so endpoint able counts must not be called survival or mortality rates. A settlement total also does not prove that every individual has access to its stock.

Strict useful chains use `soul.outcome-chain.v2` within `soul.metrics.v2`. Every controller receives the same terminal dependency test and positive-return requirement. Migration preserves v1 counts and samples as `legacyHeuristicChains`, resets strict chain totals and records their new `strictChainsSince` time. Learned execution completions remain a separate diagnostic. Earlier heuristic totals cannot support a strict-chain acceptance claim, and summed deprivation counts overlapping need conditions separately.

## Dictionary, storylets and neural gaps

The dictionary and lexicons reduce manual source writing. They supply terms, definitions, relationships and pronunciation evidence. The model still needs examples connecting terms to observations and consequences. A definition of an axe does not establish that the model recognizes a visible axe or understands its availability and ownership.

Storylets and personal outcomes create drafts. Generic decision records preserve pre-action evidence and exact available proposals behind short labels. The compiler checks those identities, keeps source/episode groups together and rejects altered targets. Candidate training never automatically replaces serving weights. Shared training also needs future eligibility rules and tests for private facts; personal memory separation alone does not prove that a model trained on approved personal content cannot reveal it.

The measured new decision fixture has 37 training, 5 validation and 3 test rows. After two GPU updates it scored **20% validation accuracy**, also **20% with missing evidence**. Its promotion gate failed and serving weights remained unchanged. This verifies the workflow and rejects an inadequate candidate; it does not demonstrate an effective neural civilization policy.

The explicit v3 controlled model now passes 120/120 validation choices and 47/48 untouched test choices for needs, resource availability and tool condition, with 50% missing-evidence accuracy and 60/60 opposed-evidence validation pairs. It was trained from browser random weights for 150 updates. The [workflow guide](soul.md#grounded-v3-model-26-september-2026) records numerical checks, artifact paths and limits. This result does not establish a complete civilization policy.

The earlier six-home-command model remains a separate result: 48/48 additional fixed-goal choices, 2/6 untrained paraphrases and 6/6 fixed-goal choices with missing observations. The legacy models do not establish dependence on perceived evidence. The v3 fixture establishes dependence on structured needs, stock and tool evidence, while live visual perception remains unverified. Legacy v2 live inputs retain bounded spatial features and compressed memory. Explicit v3 adds 64 personal features and 96 features per considered action, including own needs/goods/tools, accepted commitments and permitted target evidence. Personal camera crops/OCR are not yet feeding the normal live decision loop; imported images use an 8 by 8 grayscale representation.

The current paired-view alignment objective pulls matching representations together. Distinguishing nonmatching scenes, explicit object identity/containment and observer-relative relation targets are still needed. Add paired versus single-view baselines, unseen viewpoints, occlusion and contradictory evidence. The 192-byte-token context bounds current shortlists; richer context or architecture requires a new explicit measured version.

### Research that informs this recommendation

| Primary source | Relevant finding | Application to SOUL |
|---|---|---|
| [Voyager, Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291) | Combines an automatic curriculum, reusable executable skills and feedback from execution. It uses an existing large model without weight updates. | Reuse verified skills and choose attainable new challenges. This does not demonstrate equivalent ability in SOUL's tiny browser model. |
| [Video PreTraining, Baker et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11795) | A learned behavioral prior helped Minecraft agents reach long action sequences that were difficult from scratch. | Provide basic action competence and demonstrations before asking for complex independent production. Its training scale is not an 8 GB browser budget. |
| [Generative Agents, Park et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442) | Evaluates observation, retrieved memory, reflection and planning in a 25-agent social sandbox using a large language model. | Test personal memory and communicated plans as separate components. Believable interactions do not establish a conserved, self-sufficient economy. |
| [Crafter, Hafner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06780) | Uses survival, resource discovery and crafting achievements to evaluate a range of capabilities. | Measure concrete milestones and transfer instead of using fluent dialogue or training loss as the civilization score. |
| [Melting Pot 2.0, Agapiou et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13746) | Evaluates agents with unfamiliar social partners and mixed cooperative/competitive incentives. | Test sharing, scarcity and conflict with varied partners and seeds, including alternatives to cooperation. |
| [Accumulating Real and Synthetic Data, Gerstgrasser et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01413) | In its studied settings, retaining original data alongside accumulated synthetic data avoided collapse seen with replacement. | Preserve reviewed originals and validate continued training. This supports a data-retention strategy, not a guarantee for SOUL. |

These are design inferences from the cited research. Neural inference, loss, backpropagation and optimizer updates in SOUL remain WebGPU work. JavaScript continues to own preparation, simulation, scheduling and files. The immediate goal is a measured skill and decision model; broad conversation and social competence need their own data and evaluation.

## Verification and remaining acceptance gates

Real Chrome/Blackwell checks establish bounded capabilities, not an autonomous civilization success claim:

- The latest personal outcome fixture prefers a repeatedly useful action in **184 of 200** subsequent choices, preserves individual RNG and private evidence, revises failures and requires delivered teaching. Source: `tests/soul/latest-discovery-result.json`.
- The native app mounts 100 founders with 3,371 rendered pieces and 83 draws in the measured initial scene. It verifies both OS-owned views, lazy model allocation, archiving 100 people before creating an eight-person camp, actual GPU proposal scoring, project restoration and cleanup.
- Founder interruption tests cover rest/work exclusivity, mid-work restore, failed-write rollback, original receipt continuation, cancellation and corrupt pending evidence rejection.
- Family capability tests use explicit relationship and due-date fixtures. They verify mutual acceptance, a no-home child with fresh discovery, conserved care/theft effects, observed fighting consequences and teaching boundaries. They do not claim spontaneous multi-generation success.
- Shelter capability fixtures physically build and traverse two floors, restore partway through stairs, move household furniture and reject conflicting placement. They do not prove that the personal or neural controller independently chose and completed that whole sequence.

## Learning slice: implementation and acceptance

Research review and implementation: 26 September 2026. The six implementation areas below now have browser code and verification paths. Their empirical acceptance gates remain separate claims: controlled skill or model tests do not establish unforced civilization competence. The existing 100-person project stays supported; eight-person worlds make the independent trials inspectable.

Focused tests now demonstrate transfer to another object or location and recovery through selected interruptions; unforced behavior remains a separate acceptance gate. Roles, schedules and house assignments remain unassigned. Basic movement and physical interactions remain provided capabilities.

### Acceptance gates and interpretation

These numbers are initial engineering targets. Set them before evaluation and report failures; they are not published research thresholds.

- **Transfer:** at least 100 held-out skill trials across 20 scene seeds, covering food/water access, tool acquisition or replacement, and material delivery to accepted construction. Target at least 80% verified completion overall and in every task, within eight choices and 180 simulation seconds. Report first-choice correctness separately and account for tasks sharing a seed. Separate these focused goal-conditioned trials from free-choice society trials.
- **Independent behavior:** run eight founders on the same 20 held-out seeds for a declared, resource-feasible horizon with no forced successful choices, assigned jobs or supplied plans. Report per-person minimum health, ever-incapacitated state and deprivation plus voluntarily completed milestones, including failures. Compare random legal choices, current personal outcomes and the new controller with matched evidence and supplies. The implemented gate requires the upper bound of the paired deprivation-difference interval to be at most zero and the lower bound of the useful-chain improvement interval to exceed zero against each baseline. Count useful chains from the same actual owner receipts for every controller. Learned-chain execution remains diagnostic. Summed deprivation can count overlapping need conditions; endpoint health is not a mortality measure.
- **Adaptation:** exhaust a known resource, break a tool, move an item, interrupt work and obstruct an approach. Target at least 80% recovery in cases with an observable feasible alternative; impossible cases must terminate or change objective within the declared attempt budget.
- **Grounding:** use matched cases whose correct choice changes when needs, ownership, stock or tool condition change. Report full-evidence, missing-evidence and shuffled-evidence results with chance/majority/constant-first baselines. Test the native collection path and target-position coverage. Renaming irrelevant IDs and permuting object/candidate order must preserve the selected semantic action for deterministic inference.
- **Integrity:** no duplicated goods or effects, no access to hidden stocks or another Soul's private memory, no arrival labelled as productive completion, and no interrupted skill silently restarted as a new success after restore.
- **Browser budget:** report p50/p95 decision latency, completed decisions per second, queue age/depth, frame time, allocated model bytes and commit time at 8/32/100. Set a scheduling budget from the isolated baseline; the queue must remain bounded at the declared supported cadence. Earlier 63.21-second/100-decision evidence included development contention and is not an isolated benchmark.
- **Learning continuity:** export/import reproduces the next update within the declared FP32 tolerance. Promotion must preserve old skill evaluations and improve the held-out new skill set. A lower training loss alone does not pass.
